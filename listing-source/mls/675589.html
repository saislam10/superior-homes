Welcome to this beautiful ranch-style home in the desirable Elk Creek addition of Bel Aire! Featuring 5 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, and an open-concept layout, this home is perfect for everyday living and
entertaining. The spacious living room showcases a two-way gas fireplace that connects to the kitchen and hearth room. The gourmet kitchen offers granite countertops, a large island with eating bar, walk-in
pantry, and abundant cabinet space. The main-floor primary suite features a walk-in closet, dual vanities, granite countertops, and a separate tub and walk-in shower. The finished walkout/view-out basement
includes two additional bedrooms, a full bath, a large family room with a wet bar, and plenty of storage. Additional highlights include hardwood floors, a separate main-floor laundry room, an oversized
attached garage, covered patio and deck, sprinkler system, and a scenic backyard backing to a greenbelt with pond views and walking paths.
